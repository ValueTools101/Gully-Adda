Gully Adda — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

App: Gully Adda: 90s Retro Games (Android package com.valuetools101.gullyadda)

Developer: ValueTools101 · Contact: valuetools101@gmail.com

Gully Adda is a collection of classic Indian party and board games (Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi, Mafia, Name Place Animal Thing, Dots and Boxes, Ludo). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. Offline play (bots and pass & play)

When you play against bots or pass the phone between friends, everything stays on your device. No account, no internet connection and no data is sent to us. Your settings (sound, vibration, display name, blocked players) are stored only on your device.

2. Online play

Online rooms use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), operated by Google LLC.

3. Voice chat (optional, online Mafia only)

If you join voice chat in an online Mafia room, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to the other players in that room through LiveKit (LiveKit Inc.), our voice provider. We do not record or store voice audio. The app asks for microphone permission only when you choose to join voice, and the microphone is used only while you are in voice chat and allowed to speak. You can play without voice; text chat works instead.

4. What we do NOT do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No in-app purchases, coins, betting or real money.
  • No location, contacts, camera or photos.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers above (Google Firebase, LiveKit) who process it only to run the app.

5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Game state is only written by our server; other players can never read your secret role, slip or hidden answers.

6. Children

Gully Adda is not designed for children under 13 and is not directed at them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my online data. This deletes your anonymous account, display name, room memberships, chat messages and reports you made.
  • On the web: https://valuetools101.github.io/Gully-Adda/delete-account.html — or email valuetools101@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Gully Adda data". We respond within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app removes all offline data from your device.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
