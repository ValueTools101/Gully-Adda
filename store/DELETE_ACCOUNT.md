# Gully Adda — Delete your data

Gully Adda (by ValueTools101) does not use email or password accounts. Online play uses an **anonymous account** created automatically on your phone. Offline play (bots, pass & play) stores nothing on our servers.

## Option 1: in the app (instant)
1. Open **Gully Adda**.
2. Tap **Settings** on the home screen.
3. Tap **Delete my online data** and confirm.

This immediately deletes your anonymous account, display name, room memberships, chat messages and any reports you made.

## Option 2: by email
If you no longer have the app, email **valuetools101@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my Gully Adda data"**. If you can, include your **Player ID** (shown in the app under Settings) and the display name you used. We will delete the data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is deleted and what is kept
- **Deleted:** anonymous user ID, display name, blocked list, room memberships, your chat messages, reports you submitted.
- **Automatically deleted anyway:** game rooms and their chat ~24 hours after the last activity.
- **Kept:** reports *about* a player who broke the house rules may be kept for up to 12 months for safety, without your display name.
- Uninstalling the app removes all offline data from your phone.
