# Gully Adda — House Rules and Terms of Use for Online Play

**Effective date:** 26 November 2026 · Contact: valuetools101@gmail.com

Gully Adda online rooms are for friendly fun with people you invite. By creating or joining an online room you agree to these rules.

## House rules
1. **Be kind.** No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual content or spam in names, chat or voice.
2. **No cheating.** Don't use a second device or account to see secret information, don't share your Mafia role, chit or answers with other players, and don't try to tamper with the app or our servers.
3. **Out means out.** If you are eliminated in Mafia, do not give hints to living players in any way (voice, chat or outside the app).
4. **Decent names only.** Offensive display names are blocked or removed.
5. **No real money.** Gully Adda has no betting, coins or prizes. Don't use it for gambling of any kind.
6. **Respect privacy.** Don't share other people's personal information.

## Moderation
- Every player in a room can **Report** or **Block** any other player (Players list → Report / Block). Reporting also blocks them for you.
- The room host can **remove** a player from the room.
- Reports are reviewed by the developer. Players who break these rules can be removed from online play.
- Chat and names are filtered for common abusive words.

- You are responsible for what you say and do in rooms.
- You must be at least 13 years old to play online.
