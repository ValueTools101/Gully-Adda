## Why no Kenney / OpenMoji / Freesound files?
The build machine could not reach kenney.nl or Google Fonts directly (fonts came from npm instead), so sounds and art were made from scratch. They are fine to ship. Optional upgrades, if you want them later:
- Replace any `assets/sounds/*.wav` with a CC0 sound of the same name from Kenney "Interface Sounds" / "Casino Audio" (kenney.nl, CC0) and add a row above.
- To bundle OpenMoji (CC BY-SA 4.0, needs attribution) or Twemoji graphics (CC BY 4.0) instead of system emoji, add them under `apps/mobile/assets/emoji/` and credit them here.
